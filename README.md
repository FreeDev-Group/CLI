# Congo Leadership Institute (CLI)-Website Project

Welcome to the official repository of the **Congo Leadership Institute (CLI)** website, developed by the **FreeDev** team.

This project aims to deliver a modern digital platform that reflects the excellence, vision, and impact of CLI in promoting responsible leadership development in the **Democratic Republic of Congo** and the **Great Lakes region**.

---

## About the Web Project

### Website Objectives

- Present the CLI, its vision, values, and activities
- Provide access to its programs, trainings, and services
- Facilitate connection between leaders, organizations, and coaches
- Highlight testimonials and the measurable impact of CLI’s work

## About the Congo Leadership Institute (CLI)

Founded in **February 2014**, the **Congo Leadership Institute (CLI)** trains and mentors a new generation of Congolese leaders grounded in:

- Servant Leadership
- Holistic Human Development
- Sustainable Socio-Economic Transformation

### Objectives

- Equip leaders to reach their highest potential
- Support the development of the DRC and the Great Lakes region
- Build organizations that are radiant, competitive, and impactful

### Vision

To become the **reference institution** for leadership training and mentorship in the DRC and the Great Lakes region.

### Mission

To nurture leaders capable of transforming the following sectors:

- Politics
- Economy
- Business and Entrepreneurship
- Religion
- Humanitarian Work
- Science and Innovation

## CLI’s Learning Approach

- Learner-centered training
- Emphasis on **servant leadership**
- Small-group, community-based learning
- Organizational audits before interventions
- Hands-on sessions focused on measurable results
